# KEE vocabulary annex

> Generated from the KEE term registry by `python -m knoedg_kee.vocabulary`, the same
> command that regenerates `vocab/kee.ttl`. Do not edit by hand: the test suite
> regenerates this file and compares it byte for byte.

This annex lists every identifier KEE mints, of every kind, with its **standing**: the
term registry's admission verdict in public words. The term registry (Section 18.3)
records, for every KEE-owned surface, the eight admission criteria of Section 4.2 and
the verdict they produce; `vocab/kee.ttl` declares the classes, properties and
undeclared registered-legacy values and carries the same standing on each as
`skos:editorialNote "Standing: ..."`; the concept schemes in `schemas/skos/` declare the
controlled values. The specification names only a fraction of these terms, so this
annex is the one place a reader can see all of them and what each one's standing is.

The standing of a term is one of:

- **admitted** — the registry records every Section 4.2 criterion as met.
- **carried; not admitted under the term-admission test** — the term is retained and declared,
  but its registry row does not pass the test: the audit found no residual gap after
  direct reuse of prior art and ordinary profile composition. The term is carried as a
  convenience carrier, not admitted as a normative semantic primitive.
- **legacy, dual-read** — a registered legacy surface: readable under the Section 17
  compatibility window, not written anew; the registered migration says how it is read.
- **retire candidate** — the registry proposed retirement. The Disposition column records
  whether and how that was decided.

Standing is published, not acted on: this annex removes or demotes no term. A change
of standing is a governed decision recorded in a release, never an edit to this file.
The Disposition column is the registry's disposition where it is other than carried;
the Sections cited column lists the specification sections the registry's owner and
status entries cite, read against `docs/specs/KEE-Specification-v0.11.0.md`. The Definition column
is the registry's definition or, where it records none, the term's audited observable
obligation, which is what `vocab/kee.ttl` declares as its `skos:definition`.
